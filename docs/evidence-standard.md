# Evidence Standard

## Evidence classes

### Primary evidence
Direct source material such as analytics exports, platform screenshots, CRM exports, reports, dated before/after captures, received testimonials, or source documents.

### Secondary evidence
Material that supports interpretation but is not itself the original source, such as internal summaries or meeting notes.

### Unsupported
A statement with no traceable source. Unsupported claims must never be marked verified.

## Claim statuses
- `draft` — captured but incomplete
- `needs_review` — evidence exists but validation is incomplete
- `verified` — claim is supported by traceable evidence
- `rejected` — inaccurate, misleading, or unsupported

## Metrics
Every published metric should include a source reference and verification status. Do not convert a relative change into an absolute business impact unless the source supports that interpretation.

## Content generation gate
A record is content-ready only when the core claim is verified, evidence references are present, used metrics are verified, and publication/privacy permission is appropriate.
